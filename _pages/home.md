### Vision
To advance nuclear reactor science and technology through rigorous reactor analysis, computational modeling, and innovative approaches to reactor monitoring and diagnostics.

ANOA aims to develop a strong research environment that combines fundamental reactor physics, numerical methods, and computational tools to address emerging challenges in the design, operation, and safety of nuclear energy systems.

### Research
ANOA conducts research in nuclear reactor analysis, with an emphasis on computational methods and their application to advanced reactor systems. Our research is organized around three interconnected areas:

#### Advanced Reactor Modeling & Simulation

Development and application of computational models for analyzing the neutronic, kinetic, and operational behavior of advanced nuclear reactors. This includes research on small modular reactors, microreactors, high-temperature gas-cooled reactors, and other emerging reactor concepts.

#### Reactor Analysis Code Development

Development of reliable and efficient computational tools for nuclear reactor analysis. Research includes numerical methods, neutron transport and diffusion calculations, reactor kinetics, eigenvalue and fixed-source problems, and the development of specialized simulation codes for reactor physics applications.

#### Neutron Noise & Reactor Diagnostics

Investigation of neutron fluctuations as a means of understanding and monitoring reactor behavior. Research includes neutron-noise modeling, reactor component and fuel-assembly vibrations, Green's-function methods, source reconstruction and unfolding, detector response, and computational approaches for reactor diagnostics.

You can find an overview of some of our research themes at our [Research page](research).


### Scientific environment
We are a dynamic research group at the Department of Nuclear Engineering, [Faculty of Defense Engineering and Technology](https://ftm.idu.ac.id/), [Republic of Indonesia Defense University](https://www.idu.ac.id/), Indonesia, working at the intersection of nuclear reactor physics, computational science, and advanced reactor technology.

Our research environment brings together researchers and students with diverse backgrounds and interests in reactor analysis, numerical modeling, scientific computing, and nuclear energy systems. We value a combination of fundamental understanding, rigorous computational methods, and practical engineering applications.

ANOA actively engages in research collaborations with academic institutions, government research organizations, and international research partners. Through these collaborations, we seek to exchange knowledge, develop new computational methodologies, and address challenging problems in nuclear reactor science and technology.
