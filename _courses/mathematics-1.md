## Course Description

Mathematics I is the first course in the mathematics sequence within the Nuclear Engineering Study Program at the Republic of Indonesia Defense University. The course focuses on calculation and problem-solving, with less emphasis on theorem proofs. The curriculum covers number systems, functions and mathematical models, limits, derivatives, and integrals. Consequently, graduates are equipped to understand and apply mathematical concepts when analyzing system models.

## Learning Objectives

After completing this course, students should be able to:

1. Understand and explain the concepts of mathematical functions and models, number systems, limits, derivatives, and integrals;
2. Be capable of performing mathematical analysis using mathematical methods.

## References

1. Stewart, J. (2020). Calculus: Early Transcendentals (9th ed.). Thomas Brooks/Cole.
2. Kreyszig, E. (2011). Advanced Engineering Mathematics (10th ed.). Wiley.
